Problem Statement

India has over 14 crore demat account holders, yet many retail investors lack the knowledge and tools required to make informed investment decisions. Most investors rely on tips, fail to track important financial updates, and struggle to interpret stock market trends and technical indicators.

Although platforms like Economic Times Markets provide large amounts of financial data, the information is often complex and not easily actionable for beginner and intermediate investors.

This creates a need for an intelligent system that transforms raw financial data into simplified, personalized, and actionable insights to assist Indian investors in making better investment decisions.


Scope of the Project

This project focuses on building an AI-powered decision-support system that:

Collects stock market data from available sources

Analyzes trends and volatility

Understands user investment preferences

Generates simple and explainable investment recommendations

The system is limited to providing decision support and does not perform actual trading or guarantee financial outcomes.


Target Users

Beginner investors with limited market knowledge

Intermediate investors seeking simplified insights

College students and young professionals entering the stock market

Individuals who rely on tips and want data-driven guidance


High-Level Features

Stock data retrieval and processing

Trend analysis (uptrend/downtrend detection)

Risk assessment based on volatility

User profiling (risk level, budget, duration)

AI-based recommendation system (Buy / Hold / Avoid)

Explainable insights for each recommendation

Simple and user-friendly output/dashboard
